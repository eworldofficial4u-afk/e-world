Weapons & attachments +

All normal attachments are allowed unless specifically banned. Exploit or glitch-based attachments and mechanics are prohibited. Weapon restrictions remain consistent throughout the tournament.

Players & substitutes +

Maximum roster: five starting players and up to two substitutes. Only registered players may participate. No player may represent two teams. Substitutions are permitted only between maps unless an organizer approves otherwise. Account sharing is prohibited.

Connection & disconnects +

Restart the lobby when a disconnect happens before the first round. After play begins, continue unless an organizer confirms a lobby or server issue. Intentional disconnection intended to force a restart may result in a penalty.

Bugs & glitches +
  • Getting outside the playable map
  • Using invisible or unintended spots
  • Shooting through unintended geometry
  • Abusing bugs to plant or defuse
  • Any exploit providing an unfair advantage
Screenshots, evidence & protests +

The winning captain should retain the final scoreboard. Both teams should preserve evidence of suspicious incidents. Only captains may lodge complaints, within 10–15 minutes after the match, with screenshots or video whenever possible. Organizer decisions are final.

No-show & communication +

Teams receive a 10-minute grace period. A team without the required players after that time forfeits. Captains must monitor official E-WORLD tournament announcements.

Toxicity & sportsmanship +

Racist or hateful slurs, personal threats, harassment, excessive toxicity, match-fixing, and intentional throwing are prohibited. Penalties may include a warning, round penalty, map penalty, or disqualification.
